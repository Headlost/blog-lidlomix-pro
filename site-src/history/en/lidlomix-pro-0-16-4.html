Settings and shortcuts in 0.16.4

Since version 0.15.3, I've changed the way the robot is set on the screen. Minutes and seconds scroll separately, temperature and speed move to the side, and after setting everything, just press Start. The picture of the dish can be developed across the screen, and from the scales, Turbo and Washback to the same recipe step.

Temperature and speed moved to the side

The first editor had a Smart-inspired circle. After rehearsals, I thickened the border and expanded the area of touch. When choosing a specific temperature or gear, the level of finger movement was more convenient. The selected value is large and visible in the middle, and on the sides you can see the adjacent settings. Moving left raises the value, right lowers it. I removed the extra list under the wheel.

Time in two columns and one Start

Touch the time field, then move the minutes or seconds up or down separately. The displacement of one column leaves the other unchanged. The physical knob regulates the active column. In long programs such as fermentation, columns show hours and minutes. Changes to editable fields apply immediately. You can go from time to temperature and press Start without separate approval settings.

In Manual settings, the screen shows a 15-minute limit at speed 10. On the Connect, this limit applies to speeds 7–10. When heating is selected, a message shows that the maximum is speed 3. The same limit applies to reverse rotation. Where the settings allow it, the manual timer accepts up to 99 minutes and 99 seconds. Automatic programmes have their own ranges.

Reader programs and direction of turnover

One touch switches direction. When turning to the right, you can see a blade with arrows, and when turning to the left, you can see a flap with arrows. I've added about a third of the parameters and variants of the dough. Hard, soft and liquid cakes have their own graphics. The program header shows a small icon from the menu.

In egg cooking, I enlarged the descriptions of size, degree of cooking and time. In each row one blue field is active, the others have a white background. In steam cooking, the Heat option before counting has a larger area and a distance from Start and Stop. A similar interval is confirmed by the assumption of an appetizer and cover for cutting vegetables.

Full picture of dishes and shorter tiles

In an open recipe, touch the photo strap twice to see the whole picture in proportion. One touch closes the preview and returns to the recipe. The gesture works in the offline catalogue Monsieur Cuisine and in the Recipes Adapted Pro. In the Pro directory, the card reserves three lines for the title, and the full name is visible when it is opened.

Turbo, Weight and Wash as full programs

Turbo and Weight are next to Start in your own Settings. You can also open a wash from the floating menu. Each shortcut shows the same full screen as a separate program. Return to the recipe restores the current step and Return to the program retains the preset parameters. To use Turbo, you need to hold the button on the program screen.

  2. 0.15.5 - Switching turns with one touch and a separate choice of time unit to adjust the knob. In this series of works there were also bigger icons, variants of cakes and eggs, clearer descriptions and larger fields of options for steam and appetizers.
  3. 0.15.6 — Full-screen preview of a recipe photo in both catalogues.
  4. 0.15.7 — lower tiles in the Pro Adapted Recipes, with three lines per title.
  5. 0.15.8 — round time fields with unit selection and + and − buttons.
  6. 0.15.9 — a separate large time editor with a border on the right and a choice of unit.
  7. 0.16.0 — Extended custom settings up to 99 min and 99 sec, centered min and sec buttons, and support of this range in Connect module.
  8. 0.16.1 — more than twice the size of a large editor’s perimeter and a wider area that responds to a short drag.
  9. 0.16.2 — Vertical Time Columns, Instant Settings, Start Under Editor, Limit Messages and Turbo Shortcuts and Weight in Own Settings.
  10. 0.16.3 — horizontal temperature and speed selectors, large digits and removing the additional set-up list.
  11. 0.16.4 — Full screens of shortcut tools and return to the same program or recipe step.

Tests on Connect

On the device I checked scrolling in both time columns, the 15:00 limit at speed 10, the heating message, the horizontal controls and the return from quick tools. On the test unit I also looked into a scale reading stuck at 0 g. Trying the older Connect module and disconnecting the power did not restore a response to a 1 kg bag. The cause remains unconfirmed.
